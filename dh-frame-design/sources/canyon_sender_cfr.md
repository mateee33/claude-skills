## Generation and configuration
- Current generation: Gen 3 (platform code M180), high pivot with idler, launched 6 May 2025. [M]
  https://www.canyon.com/en-de/mountain-bikes/downhill-bikes/sender/
  https://media-centre.canyon.com/en-INT/248858-all-new-canyon-sender-cfr-downhill-bike-unveiled-with-high-pivot-suspension-design/
- Models on sale Nov 2026: Sender CFR CLLCTV (ID 4447) and Sender CFR Underdog (ID 4448); MY2025 were Team (3655) and Underdog (3651). Geometry table, frame weight, shock size and axle are identical on all four product pages (inference: same frame; Canyon does not state it). [M]
- Wheel configuration: mullet only, 29" front / 27.5" rear. [M]
- Sizes S, M, L, XL. No frame-only sale. [M]

Other settings:
- Low BB: head angle 62.7°, BB height 347 mm (high: 63°, 352 mm). [M press release]
- Reach cups ±8 mm. Size M short/long = 460/476 mm [I, The Loam Wolf]; size S long = 451 mm, wheelbase 1254 mm [I, BikeRadar]. L and XL cup positions: NOT FOUND (±8 mm implied).
- Chainstay 438 mm static, all sizes, not adjustable; ~455.5 mm at sag [I-rep].
- Axle path ~24 mm rearward to ~70 % travel, then ~5 mm forward [I-rep].
- Fork offset 52 mm; front travel 200 mm (BoXXer) / 203 mm (Fox 40); rear travel 200 mm per Canyon (204 mm in some spec lists — conflict). Fork axle-to-crown assumed in chart: NOT FOUND.
- Progression chip 32.7 % / 37 %. [M] Leverage-ratio curve, start/end ratios: NOT FOUND.
- Anti-rise: "130 %" [M, no travel point]; 124 % at sag [I-rep Loam Wolf]. Anti-squat ~125 % at sag [I-rep]. Pedal kickback 0.5°, chain growth 0.1 mm [I-rep, attributed to Canyon].

## Suspension hardware
- Layout: high single pivot, linkage-driven, idler in line with main pivot [M; I BikeRadar].
- Shock 250 x 75 mm [M]; mount type not stated (drawings show bolted upper eyelet in flip-chip plate).
- Stock shocks are coil (RockShox Vivid Coil / Fox DHX2). Recommended sag 30 % [M Quick Start Guide p.17].

## Weight, limits, category
- Frame weight claimed 4614 g, "based on size M"; with/without shock NOT STATED. [M] Other figures 4680 g (Pinkbike), 4720 g (Vital) [2nd]. Independent measurement: NOT FOUND.
- Complete bike: Team 17.92 kg (M) claimed [M]; Team size L measured 18.38 kg [I ENDURO]; Underdog 18.52 kg measured without pedals [I BIKE].
- Max system weight: generic Canyon MTB manual 120 kg [M]; BIKE data box 130 kg for the Sender [I-rep].
- Category 5 rated [M]. Manual gives no numeric drop/jump limit for Category 5. Guarantee excludes "damage resulting from jumps or other types of overstress" [M MTB manual p.119].

## Hardware standards
- Rear axle 12x148 Boost (custom DT hub with DH-157 internal flange spacing) [M; I]. Front 20x110.
- BB threaded BSA 83. Headset standard NOT FOUND (reach cups, 1 1/8" steerer). Rear rotor 200 or 220 mm.
- Tyre clearance 63 mm. Seatpost 34.9 mm.

## Independent tests (no stiffness or timed data found)
- BikeRadar 08.01.2026 (Team, S): not lively; manuals/hops take effort; complicated frame. https://www.bikeradar.com/reviews/bikes/mountain-bikes/full-suspension-mountain-bikes/canyon-sender-cfr-team
- ENDURO 18.09.2025 (Team, L, 18.38 kg): heaviest analogue bike in group; composed on jump landings; complex maintenance. https://enduro-mtb.com/en/canyon-sender-cfr-team-review/
- BIKE 10.11.2025 (Underdog): sluggish in tight bends, "less pop for jumps". https://www.bike-magazin.de/en/bicycles/mountain-bike/downhill-bikes-or-freeride-bikes/canyon-sender-cfr-underdog-on-test-this-big-bike-wants-to-inspire-for-little-money/
- The Loam Wolf 06.05.2025 (Team, M): wallows mid-stroke when set soft. https://theloamwolf.com/reviews/bikes/first-ride-review-canyon-sender-gen-3
- Pinkbike [2nd]: 438 mm rear centre felt rear-biased; wants size-specific rear ends.
