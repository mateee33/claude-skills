## Suspension
- Layout: "HIGH VIRTUAL CONTACT SYSTEM", 6-bar virtual high pivot with idler. MFR
- Shock 250 x 75 mm, bushings 30x8 / 30x8 mm. Average leverage 220/75 = 2,93 (derived). Recommended sag 20–25 % / 15–19 mm. MFR
- Commencal publishes no leverage, anti-squat, anti-rise or axle-path numbers.
- Independent estimate, Linkage Design (A. Osuna, July 2023), modelled from a product photo, chip position not stated:
  https://linkagedesign.blogspot.com/2023/07/commencal-supreme-dh-2023.html
  leverage ≈3.45 → ≈2.6 (≈24–25 % progression); anti-rise ≈93 % at sag (≈99 % at 0 → ≈69 % at 220 mm);
  axle path max ≈14 mm rearward at ≈115–120 mm, ≈−1 mm at 220 mm; anti-squat 34/14 ≈83 % at sag; kickback ≈−5° at 220 mm.
  Graph readings ±1–2 units. **Third-party estimate, not measured, not manufacturer data.**
- Commencal setup chart (V5, MFR): 80 kg rider → Float X2 250 psi, BoXXer 145 psi. Chart spans 50–110 kg.

## Weight, limits, classification
- Frame weight: 5,3 kg (V5), size and shock inclusion not stated. V5.2: 5,8 kg. MFR. Independent measurement: NOT FOUND.
- Complete V5 (size S, no pedals) 17,5–18,7 kg claimed. Blister measured 18.5 kg, size L, Öhlins build [snippet].
- Max rider weight fully equipped 120 kg (general manual). MFR
- Intended use "Downhill Racing"; Commencal manual Category 4 ("higher speed and extreme use (big jump, bikepark...)"). MFR
- EFBE Category 5 certification is claimed for V5.2 only; no such claim found for V5. MFR
- Warranty (V5: 2 years frame) does not apply "in case of use in competitions" or to "damage or breakage from jumps or falls". MFR
  https://www.commencal.com/en/warranty-terms-bikes.html

## Independent tests (no stiffness or timed data found)
- FREERIDE/BIKE 14.09.2024 six-bike test: top score; minus: weight, long chainstays; preferred High + Progressive.
  https://www.bike-magazin.de/fahrraeder/mountainbike/downhill-bikes-freeride-bikes/commencal-supreme-dh-v5-benchmark-der-high-end-downhiller/

## Red Bull Rampage
- No evidence found of a Supreme DH V5/V5.2 ridden at Rampage. Documented Commencal Rampage bikes are Furious or FRS
  (2026: FRS, 200 mm, 27.5" front and rear). Commencal's V5.2 FAQ: "Is it playful in freeride? No… the FRS and CLASH models are much more suitable." MFR
  https://www.commencal.com/en/FT7SUPV521.html
- Red Bull's Rampage bikes page (read directly, updated 7 Oct 2026): riders "prefer stiffer suspension all around" than World Cup DH;
  tyres 35–45 psi; wheels "tend to run smaller" (27.5", 26" or mixed); mostly tubes; most bikes run 200 mm rotors. IND
  https://www.redbull.com/us-en/red-bull-rampage-bikes
